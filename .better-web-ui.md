# HogarIA design context

## People and jobs

- Audience: household members who plan meals and cook at mixed experience levels, including beginners.
- Core recipe jobs: browse, create and edit recipes; choose beginner/intermediate/expert instructions; adjust servings; cook step by step with clear tips, warnings, timers, nutrition and storage guidance.
- Most sensitive context: mobile use in the kitchen, often one-handed; desktop is used for planning and editing.

## Product feel

- Calm, practical, warm, trustworthy and non-judgmental; Spanish (Spain) copy by default.
- Preserve HogarIA's existing orange-led palette, typography, tokens, shared controls and shell. Do not invent a second design system for recipes.
- Reference: the selected Tasty step-by-step pattern for focused instructions and short, optional, licensed media—not its brand styling.
- Quality bar: production-ready and meticulously aligned. Equivalent controls share dimensions, icon treatment, spacing and states across routes and breakpoints.

## Interaction defaults

- Use tabs/subtabs to keep editing and cooking flows focused; do not stack every section into one long screen.
- Keep the global lateral navigation; avoid mobile bottom navigation.
- Provide keyboard/focus support, touch targets, reduced-motion and data-saving behavior. Media is optional and never blocks recipe content.
- AI output is a reviewable draft. Explicit user action applies it; explicit Save persists it.
